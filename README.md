# ACLS Simulator

A mobile-first ACLS megacode simulator in one file (`index.html`). No build tools: open the file in any browser.

**Version 1** has one case, VF / pulseless VT arrest, with two switches:

- Pause and explain, or real-time megacode
- Ideal AHA setup, or low-resource (monophasic defibrillator, no pacer, no capnography, bag-mask only, no amiodarone or adenosine)

You are the team leader. At the end you get a score out of 100 with the guideline reason for each point.

Source: Wigginton JG, et al. Part 9: Adult Advanced Life Support: 2025 American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and Emergency Cardiovascular Care. Circulation 2025;152(16 suppl 2):S538-S577. doi:10.1161/CIR.0000000000001376

For training only. Not a medical device.
